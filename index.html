PAC-MAN

ARCADE 1980 CLASSIC
🎮 Controls: Arrow Keys or W/A/S/D • Space: Pause • M: Sound • R: Restart

HOW TO PLAY

🎯 Objective

Guide Pac-Man through the maze, eating all pellets and energizers while avoiding the four ghosts!

👻 Ghost Personalities

BLINKY (Red)

Shadow: Direct chaser. Targets Pac-Man's exact location. Accelerates as dots decrease (Cruise Elroy).

PINKY (Pink)

Speedy: The ambusher. Targets 4 spaces ahead of Pac-Man to cut off escape routes.

INKY (Cyan)

Bashful: Tactical flanker. Targets the symmetric reflection across Blinky and Pac-Man.

CLYDE (Orange)

Pokey: The coward. Chases Pac-Man when far away, but flees to his corner when getting too close.

🍒 Scoring

  • Pellet (Dot): 10 pts
  • Energizer (Power Pellet): 50 pts (frightens ghosts)
  • Frightened Ghosts: 200, 400, 800, 1600 pts (combo chain)
  • Bonus Fruits: 100 to 5,000 pts (Cherry, Strawberry, Peach, Apple, Melon, Galaxian, Bell, Key)
  • Extra Life: Awarded at 10,000 pts
